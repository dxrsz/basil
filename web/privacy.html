Privacy Policy

Lamar's Groceries · Last updated 5 October 2026

Lamar's Groceries is a shared grocery list with meal planning. This page explains what the app stores, why, who helps run it, and how you can download or delete your data. The short version: we keep what's needed to run your lists, we don't sell anything, there are no ads or trackers, and the AI features are off until you turn them on.

Who we are

Lamar's Groceries is run by Evan Rose, who is the data controller for the app. For any privacy question or request, email privacy@lamarsgroceries.app.

We don't collect your location, contacts or advertising IDs. There's no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. Photos you import are processed to read the items and are not stored.

AI helpers (OpenAI)

Meal ideas, ingredient checks, meal pictures, list tidying and photo imports use OpenAI. They only run if you turn on AI helpers, which the app asks about once and which you can change any time in Account. When you use one, the meal names, list items, taste notes or photo involved are sent to OpenAI to get an answer. Your email and name are never sent. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data isn't used to train their models and is kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.

We never sell or share your data for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and lists: until you delete them or your account.
  • Meal history (used for "make it again" and ratings): one year.
  • AI request counts: two days.
  • Expired invite links: removed 30 days after they expire.
  • "Someone's shopping" notices: one day.
  • Backups made by our database provider may hold deleted data for a short time until they roll over.

Your rights

You can do most of this yourself in the app under Account:

  • Access and portability: "Download my data" gives you everything as a JSON file.
  • Correction: edit your name, lists and meals any time.
  • Deletion: "Delete account" erases your account right away. Lists only you use are deleted. Lists you share stay for the other members, and the next person on each one takes it over. Things you added to a shared list remain there for the others, without your name.
  • Withdraw consent: turn AI helpers off at any time. This doesn't affect anything already processed.
  • Objection and restriction: email us.

If you're in the EU, UK or another place with similar laws, you can also complain to your data protection authority. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every table is protected by row-level security, so only members of a list can read it. AI requests go through our server, never straight from your device, and are rate-limited.

Children

Lamar's Groceries isn't meant for children under 13 (16 in some EU countries). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
